History

White Water Rafting Company was founded on the banks of Uganda’s legendary Nile River, where powerful rapids and breathtaking scenery inspired a group of adventurers to share the thrill of rafting with the world. from our humble beginnings , we have grown into a trusted outfitter known for combining safety, expertise, and unforgettable experiences. Over the years, thousands of guests have joined us to conquer the Nile’s waves, explore hidden gorges, and create lasting memories. Our history is built on passion for adventure, respect for nature, and a commitment to making every trip extraordinary.

Together, we work tirelessly to create unforgettable memories for our guests while fostering a culture of teamwork, respect, and adventure.
